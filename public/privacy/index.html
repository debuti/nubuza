How our apps handle your data

Our apps are local-first: there are no user accounts, and what you create stays on your device or, if you turn it on, in your own Google Drive. We don't receive it, sell it or use it for advertising. Where an app needs a server for a feature (for example, sharing), its own policy says exactly what is sent and how to delete it.

Advertising

Our apps are free and paid for by ads: Google AdSense on the web and Google AdMob in the Android apps. Google and its partners may use cookies, the device's advertising ID and similar identifiers, your IP address and your interactions with the ads to serve them, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you consent, personalise them, including from your visits to other websites and apps. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland Google's consent message asks you first. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy and cookies" link at the bottom of each web app, or in Settings → Ad privacy choices in the Android apps.

Google processes this data under its own responsibility: see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. We don't control which ads are shown and we never pass your app data to the ad services.

This website

nubuza.com shows no ads, sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It loads its font from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver it.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts our sites and, like any web host, handles your IP address and technical request data to serve the pages and keep them secure; some apps also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies and without identifying you. Google provides the ads, the optional Drive sync and Google Play. Each one follows its own privacy policy.

Children

Our apps are not aimed at children under 14.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to data portability. Write to me@nubuza.com. Since your data lives on your device or in your own Drive, you can also view, copy or delete it yourself at any time. If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
